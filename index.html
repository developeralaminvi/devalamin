FAQ

Answered Questions

Everything you might want to know—straightforward and transparent.

What is your typical turnaround time for a project?
+

For single landing pages or redesigns, turnaround is typically 3–5 business days. For full multi-page corporate sites or complex WooCommerce setups, projects usually take 1–2 weeks, with daily progress updates.

Do you build custom themes or use page builders like Elementor?
+

Both! I specialize in Elementor Pro for clients who want easy drag-and-drop editing themselves, but I also build completely custom WordPress themes using ACF Pro and Gutenberg blocks for lightweight, ultra-fast performance.

How do you guarantee a 90+ Google PageSpeed score?
+

I optimize every asset directly: converting images to WebP/AVIF, eliminating render-blocking CSS/JS, minifying code, configuring LiteSpeed / WP Rocket server caching, optimizing database queries, and setting up CDN caching on Cloudflare.

Can you recover a hacked WordPress site or fix critical 500 errors?
+

Yes. I have rescued hundreds of hacked websites: removing injected malicious scripts/backdoors, fixing white-screen-of-death (WSOD) issues, resolving database connection failures, and putting in place robust firewall rules.

What do you need from me to get started?
+

Simply reach out with your website link or design concept (Figma, PSD, or reference URLs). If you have hosting/domain access, that's great; if not, I can recommend the best hosting setup tailored to your budget and traffic.
